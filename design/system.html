DESIGN SYSTEM · #522

Oriel's design system

What Oriel borrows from Hoplet is the method: every value has a layer, a name and a source, code takes only tokens, and a contrast that misses its target cannot merge. The look stays Oriel's own: paper and ink, an ink pane set into the window like a bay window, one quiet teal, and type that serves long reading. The values live in design/tokens.json and design/layout.json; this board draws them.

What we borrow, what we keep

Left: the method taken from Hoplet. Right: what Oriel keeps and does not trade for it.

The method

  • design/tokens.json and design/layout.json are the one source; go generate writes internal/ui/theme from them; tokens.md only says why
  • Color in layers: region → surface → state → role → text → meaning → line, and every token names its layer
  • Text colors come from contrast targets, not taste; the audit in tokens.json is computed by a test, and a miss cannot merge
  • Named text styles (11), radii by role, a size and spacing scale and motion durations are all tokens
  • A guard test fails on a literal size, ui.Hex or RGBA, or a duration in a view
  • Layout patterns have names (bar, entry-row, column, stack-*…); parts carry no outer margin; only a spacer pushes to the end

Oriel's character

  • Paper and ink: warm paper in light (hue about 38°), graphite with a hint of teal in dark; never pure white or black, never a hueless gray, no glass
  • The ink pane: the sidebar inset 8 with radius 14, warm charcoal in light and deep teal in dark: the oriel the app is named after; now one region with its own text tiers
  • One teal: a muted #2a6d65 / #78beb1 that does not follow the system accent; amber only for the star
  • Reading first: the article body is a sixth, separate scale set by the reading preferences; Sepia and Mist replace the page region whole
  • Quiet motion: color and opacity, a popup's small scale, a fold; no number rolls or staggers
  • Same on three platforms: opaque everywhere, one palette on macOS, Windows and Linux

Color in layers

Each color says only which layer it is on and what it does. Contrast is computed live on the page: text on its worst bed (paper, surface, float and both with the selected fill; the pane and the pane with its selected fill). The state ladder is now fill < hover < selected < pressed, so pressing a chosen row still shows.

Text styles

The interface uses these 11 styles and no others. Hierarchy comes from lightness first (fg → fg2 → muted), then weight, then size. Simplified Chinese gets taller lines. Changing numbers use tabular numerals. The article body and its headings are the sixth scale, set by the reading preferences, and are not listed here.

Layout: the timeline

One skeleton for an entry row: meta line (site 16, source, time) → title → two lines of summary → media. Rows lie flat on paper with no card ground; a hairline runs from the text's start to the right padding; hover fills, the chosen row takes selected and the 2.5 × 18 teal marker; unread is the teal dot before the title. Videos and pictures are large media in the row; articles take a thumbnail at the end.
